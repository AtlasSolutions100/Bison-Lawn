Scheduling
Waiting List
This is where we schedule timeframes for when work is to be done (ex. March 1 to April 30), but it’s not scheduled officially yet. Then, from the waiting list, the user can dispatch the job and pick a date and it gets converted into real work to be done.
We need to be able to click on a date on the waiting list and have all of the items that are in that potential date show up.
Dispatch Job
This is where jobs live and there are statuses in here that show if they are completed, need to be completed today, or “not yet visible to sub contractor” as the 3 statuses.
The dispatch and waiting list boards are effectively like a CRM except for jobs, rather than customers. Headers for the columns in the waiting list / dispatch boards: Order (Routing, what comes first), Status, Client (Name & Address), Account Balance, Service, Date, Map (text notes is all it really is, custom written by the user), City, Assigned (Subs), Last Service, Budgeted Hours, Amount, Notes, Products (which is it’s own object and can be multiple things and the prices associated with them)
