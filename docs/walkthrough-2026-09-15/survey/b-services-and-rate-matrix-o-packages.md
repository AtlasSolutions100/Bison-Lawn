In AddServiceDialog (client-detail.tsx:1116-1300) read the client's saved value (pass the `client` object or `useGetClient(clientId)`), label the box with the pricing field label (reuse `pricingUnitLabel` pattern from services.tsx:63-67) and prefill it; keep manual override. ### Risks
schedulingRules.ts creates jobs from accepted estimate lines with `amount = calculatedPrice`; with visits > 1 one job would carry the whole season total — decide whether acceptance creates `visits` jobs or one job at unit price (recommend: one client_service with quantity=visits and jobs at unitPrice; document it).
